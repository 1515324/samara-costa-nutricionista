## Brand & Style
The design system balances the clinical authority of premium private healthcare with the tactile intimacy of high-end editorial wellness. It distances itself from aggressive fitness trackers and generic corporate clinics by evoking an atmosphere of calm, thoughtful, and bespoke nutritional science.

The target audience consists of discerning, health-conscious private patients seeking sustainable well-being, metabolic optimization, or clinical dietary care. The UI must project empathy, supreme competence, and discretion.

The aesthetic marries **Editorial Warmth** with **Modern Organic Minimalism**:
- Generous, breathable canvas space reminiscent of editorial publications.
- Restrained color harmony drawn from herbal botanicals, raw linen, and terracotta earth.
- Tactile, deliberate micro-interactions that reassure instead of overstimulate.
- Immediate conversion affordances (frictionless lead capture, frictionless WhatsApp triggers) embedded seamlessly into an environment of clinical confidence.

## Colors
The color palette avoids sterile clinical whites and harsh neon accents, establishing authority through deep botanical greens and reassurance through warm earth tones:

- **Primary (`#24352A` - Deep Forest Olive):** Used for commanding typography, primary structural headers, high-authority UI containers, and highest-contrast interactive anchors.
- **Secondary (`#385040` - Sage Laurel):** The core active color. Applied to high-conversion buttons, verified credentials, progress rings, and focal focal interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#C67D5A` - Soft Terracotta):** The conversion warmth accent. Applied strictly to urgent conversion cues, limited consultation tags, micro-badges, and conversion highlights without compromising medical credibility.
- **Support Accent (`#D3C5B4` - Warm Oat / Gilded Linen):** Subtle dividing lines, refined tag backgrounds, and surface stroke accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F8F5` - Pale Raw Linen):** Foundational surface tone that reduces eye strain and removes clinical coldness.
- **Neutral Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Bone):** Used inside raised conversion cards, form inputs, and clinical evidence modules to create natural, soft elevation against the linen background.
- **Text Body & Data (`#1A241E` - Deep Mineral Graphite):** Ultra-legible deep graphite tint with an imperceptible organic undertone to prevent jarring jet-black contrast.

## Typography
The system balances human editorial grace with crisp typographic utility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys bespoke clinical prestige, intellectual rigour, and empathy. Headings must always maintain relaxed line heights to emphasize deliberate, mindful pacing. Italic variants may be utilized sparingly for emotional emphasis in hero subtitles or quotes.
- **Body & Numerical Information (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine clarity across varying screen resolutions. Its geometric yet humane character prevents fatigue in long clinical descriptions, protocol outlines, and symptom assessments.
- **Labels, Badges, and Medical Registration Numbers (CRN):** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with slightly tracked uppercase or semi-bold weights for rapid legibility and instant authority.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by strict content containment defines the desktop layout:
- **Maximum Content Width:** 1200px for balanced reading comfort and optical alignment. Form funnels and conversion modules clamp to a focused 640px or 760px container to eliminate peripheral distraction.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter, 32px (`2rem`) outer margin. Section paddings range from 80px to 112px vertically to create an unhurried, luxury aesthetic.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutter, 24px outer margin. Split editorial blocks stack gracefully while trust credentials and doctor biography retain horizontal balance.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutter, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margin. Vertical rhythm contracts to 48px to 64px section separation, with sticky conversion triggers fixed cleanly in the thumb zone.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics fine stationery and natural architectural soft-lighting:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#F9F8F5` (Linen).
  - Raised cards & form panels: `#FFFFFF` (Bone White), bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(211, 197, 180, 0.45)`.
  - Floating CTAs & contextual overlays: Pure `#FFFFFF` with ambient warm diffusion.
- **Shadow Profiles:**
  - **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Interactive Modules):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.02)`.
  - **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.03)`.
  - **Level 3 (Floating WhatsApp Button & Booking Drawer):** `0 18px 40px -6px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Tone Over Gloss:** Do not use high-gloss reflections or aggressive neomorphism. Depth is felt through organic tinting, micro-borders, and gentle shadow diffusions.

## Shapes
The shape language relies on organic geometry that feels approachable and human, avoiding razor-sharp medical corners while preserving structure:

- **Containers & Medical Panels:** Medium roundedness (`1rem` / 16px) for major consultation cards, booking form wrappers, and before/after testimony vessels.
- **Micro UI & Interactive Fields:** Soft roundedness (`0.5rem` / 8px) for text inputs, dropdowns, and schedule selectors to ensure structured data entry.
- **Pill Geometry:** Reserved specifically for trust badges (CRN validation, certifications), status indicators ("Apenas 3 vagas este mês"), and primary conversion triggers to maximize visual affordance and tactile tapping comfort.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Conversion Button (Booking & Funnel Action):** 
  - Fill: `#385040` (Sage Laurel) with pure `#FFFFFF` typography.
  - Geometry: Pill or 12px radius, minimum height of 52px (mobile thumb-friendly).
  - Hover: Background darkens smoothly to `#24352A`, subtle upward translation of `-1px`, shadow transitions to Level 2.
- **Terracotta Focus CTA (Direct Action / Offer Highlight):**
  - Fill: `#C67D5A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Applied sparingly to direct attention without overwhelming the page.
- **Floating WhatsApp Booking Trigger:**
  - Fixed bottom-right corner (24px offset on desktop, 16px on mobile).
  - Structure: Deep emerald-olive or WhatsApp branded green (`#25D366`) paired with a pill badge stating *"Agendar Consulta Particular"*.
  - Presence: Unobtrusive initial state expanding with a subtle micro-pulse on first viewport scroll past 30%.

### Badges & Credibility Seals
- **Medical / CRN Credibility Chips:** 
  - Off-white base, bordered by `1px solid #D3C5B4`, text in `#24352A` with an organic checkmark or botanical crest icon.
  - Lettering in uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Urgency / Exclusivity Badge:**
  - Background: Pale terracotta wash (`rgba(198, 125, 90, 0.12)`), text in `#C67D5A`, font weight 600.

### Input Fields & High-Conversion Form Elements
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF` against the `#F9F8F5` canvas to create focus contrast.
  - Border: `1.5px solid #D3C5B4`.
  - Active/Focused: Border shifts to `#385040` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(56, 80, 64, 0.1)`). No standard browser outlines.
  - Labels: Floating or strictly docked atop input, rendered in `#24352A` with clear indicator for required fields.
- **Multi-Step Assessment Selectors (Symptom / Goal Chips):**
  - Selectable chips that toggle active state with `#385040` fill and white text to reduce friction compared to standard checkboxes.

### Cards & Social Proof
- **Patient Transformation & Testimonial Cards:**
  - Pure white background, `1rem` radius, fine border (`1px solid rgba(211, 197, 180, 0.5)`).
  - Includes quotation marks in muted terracotta, subtle demographic details (e.g., "Paciente acompanhada por 6 meses"), and discrete metric callouts.
- **Methodology / Consultation Pillars:**
  - Minimalist layout with clear numerical indicators (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `Playfair Display` at 30% opacity, paired with concise value copy.
